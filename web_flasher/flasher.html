Installation Options
Option A: Standard Update (Preserve Stats)
Flashes application binary at offset 0x10000. Preserves all stored click counts and local settings in NVS.
Option B: Factory Reset (Clean Install)
Performs full chip erase and writes complete image at offset 0x0. Resets device memory to factory state.
Important: Close VS Code's Serial Monitor or active terminals before clicking below to avoid COM port locking.
Web Serial is not supported on this browser. Please open in Chrome / Edge or use manual options below.
Web Serial requires HTTPS or localhost.

Flashing Instructions

HOW-TO
  • 1
    Plug in ESP32 via USB

    Connect your Clicker to your computer with a standard data USB cable.

  • 2
    Click "Connect & Flash Device"

    Select your ESP32's COM / USB Serial port from the browser prompt.

  • 3
    Complete & Restart

    The flasher writes to 0x10000. Your device reboots with full click history intact.

Tip: If connection times out, press and hold the BOOT button on your board while clicking Connect.

Option 3: esptool.py (CLI)

Flash the merged factory image directly to offset 0x0:

esptool.py --chip esp32 --port COMx write_flash 0x0 factory_firmware.bin
Or run flash_device.bat in the repository.

Manual Binary Files & Data Safety Notice

• factory_firmware.bin (Offset 0x0): All-in-one image with bootloader and partitions for web.esphome.io or factory resets.
• firmware.bin (Offset 0x10000): App-only update that preserves all lifetime click counters and milestone stats in NVS (0x9000).
• Desktop CLI: Use .\flash_device.bat to interactively choose whether to keep stats or erase.
